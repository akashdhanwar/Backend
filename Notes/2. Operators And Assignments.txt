- Increment and Decrement Operators
Includes pre and post operators

    -------------------------------------------------------------------------
    |   Expression   |   Initial(x)     |   Value of y      |   Final(x)    |
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------
    |   y = ++x;     |       10         |   11              |   11          |
    |   y = x++;     |       10         |   10              |   11          |
    |   y = --x;     |       10         |   9               |   9           |
    |   y = x--;     |       10         |   10              |   9           |
    -------------------------------------------------------------------------

    Only for variables and not for constants. Example -   int y = ++(++x);    CE: required variable, found value
    For final variables, no increment/decrement
    For every primitives except boolean
    
    Difference between b=b+1 and b++
    b = b + 1;  -   byte b = 10;
                    b = b + 1;          
                    sop(b);
                    CE: Possible loss of precision
                    b = (byte)(b+1);
    b++;        -   b = byte
                    b = (type of b)(b+1)
                    Internal Type Casting
